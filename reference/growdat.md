# Book 1 teaching cohort (simulated)

A simulated school health cohort of about 3,500 children followed
through annual examinations from 2018 to 2025 (no examinations in 2021)
at three sites, ages 18 months to 18 years. Generated by
`wgh_simulate(4000, preset = "wgh_like", seed = 2024)`. The cohort
resembles the school health cohort of the research program so analyses
give tables and figures similar to the papers. It is not real data.

## Usage

``` r
growdat
```

## Format

A data frame with one row per visit:

- pid:

  Child identifier

- sex:

  Factor, Male or Female

- site:

  Site (Site1, Site2, Site3)

- dob, examdate:

  Date of birth and of examination

- yearvisit:

  School year of the examination

- age:

  Exact age in years

- height, weight, bmi:

  cm, kg, kg/m2

- haz, waz, baz:

  WHO z-scores (weight-for-age only to 10 years)

## Source

Simulated with [`wgh_simulate()`](wgh_simulate.md). See
`data-raw/datasets.R`.
