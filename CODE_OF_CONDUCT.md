# Contributor Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short, everyone taking part in this project, as a maintainer, contributor
or user, is expected to be respectful and constructive, to welcome people of
every background and level of experience, and to accept feedback gracefully.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Problems can be reported to the maintainer at nhanhocumc@gmail.com. All
reports will be reviewed promptly and handled in confidence, and the
maintainer may remove comments or contributions, or exclude people from the
project, in line with the full text of the Covenant.
